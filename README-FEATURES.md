# 📱 CommUnity - User Features Guide

## 👋 Welcome to CommUnity!

CommUnity is your social platform for sharing photos and videos, connecting with friends, and discovering amazing content. This guide explains everything you can do in the app.

---

## 🎯 Main Features

### 1. 📝 Account Management

#### **Sign Up**
- Create your account with email and password
- Choose a unique username
- Add your full name
- Get started instantly!

#### **Login**
- Secure email/password authentication
- Your session stays active until you logout
- Quick access to your account

#### **Profile Setup**
- Add a profile picture (tap to upload from gallery or camera)
- Write a bio (up to 150 characters)
- Your username and name display on all your posts
- Edit your profile anytime from the profile tab

---

### 2. 🏠 Home Feed

The home feed is where you discover content from all users in the community.

#### **What You Can See:**
- 📸 **Photo Posts** - Beautiful images shared by users
- 🎥 **Video Posts** - Short videos (up to 15 seconds)
- 👤 **User Information** - Username and profile picture of post creator
- 💬 **Captions** - What users are saying about their posts
- ❤️ **Like Count** - See how many people liked the post
- 💬 **Comment Count** - Number of comments (coming soon)

#### **What You Can Do:**
- ✅ **Scroll Infinitely** - Keep scrolling to discover more posts
- ✅ **Pull to Refresh** - Swipe down to load new content
- ✅ **Like Posts** - Double-tap or click the heart icon
- ✅ **Unlike Posts** - Tap the filled heart to unlike
- ✅ **View User Profiles** - Tap on username or profile picture
- ✅ **Random Discovery** - Posts appear in random order for better discovery

#### **Create New Post:**
- Tap the **Camera Icon** in the top-right corner
- Choose to take a photo/video or select from gallery
- Add a caption (optional)
- Post instantly!

---

### 3. 🎬 Reels (Video Feed)

Reels is a dedicated space for watching short-form vertical videos.

#### **Video Experience:**
- 📹 **Vertical Scrolling** - Swipe up to see the next video
- 📐 **Original Aspect Ratio** - Videos display in their original dimensions (no cropping)
- ⏸️ **Background Stop** - Videos automatically stop when you leave the screen (no background audio)

#### **Video Information:**
- Username and profile picture
- Video caption
- Like count

#### **Interactions:**
- ❤️ Like videos
- 👤 View creator's profile
- 🔄 Keep scrolling for more

---

### 4. ➕ Create Post

Share your moments with the community!

#### **Upload Image:**
1. Tap the **+** button or camera icon
2. Select **"Take Photo"** or **"Choose from Gallery"**
3. **Crop & Edit** - Free-form cropping (no forced aspect ratio!)
4. Add a caption
5. Tap **"Post"**

**Image Specifications:**
- ✅ Any aspect ratio supported
- ✅ Compressed for faster upload
- ✅ Stored securely in database

#### **Upload Video:**
1. Tap the **+** button or camera icon
2. Select **"Record Video"** or **"Choose from Gallery"**
3. **Automatic Trimming:**
   - If video is longer than 15 seconds, you'll see the video editor
   - Instagram-style timeline with handles
   - Drag handles to select 15-second clip
   - See duration, size, and selected time
   - Use -1s/+1s buttons for fine adjustments
4. Add a caption
5. Tap **"Post"**

**Video Specifications:**
- ⏱️ Maximum 15 seconds
- 📦 Maximum 10MB file size
- 🎬 MP4 format recommended
- 📹 Original aspect ratio preserved

---

### 5. 👤 Your Profile

Your personal space to manage your content and settings.

#### **Profile Information:**
- Profile picture
- Username
- Full name
- Bio
- Statistics:
  - 📊 Post count
  - 👥 Followers count
  - 👥 Following count

#### **View Your Content:**
Toggle between two views:
- **Grid View** 📱 - See all your posts in a grid layout
- **Reels View** 🎬 - See only your video posts in a list

#### **Edit Profile:**
1. Tap **"Edit Profile"** button
2. Change your profile picture
3. Update your bio (max 150 characters)
4. Tap **"Save"** to update

#### **Manage Posts:**
- **View Post Details** - Tap any post to see full size
- **Play Videos** - Tap video thumbnail to watch in full screen
- **Delete Posts:**
  1. Long press on any post
  2. Select "Post Options"
  3. Choose "Delete Post"
  4. Confirm deletion
  5. Post is permanently removed

**Delete Icon:** Small trash icon overlay appears on all your posts

---

### 6. 🔍 Discover Users

Find and connect with other users in the community.

#### **View Other Profiles:**
- Tap on any username or profile picture
- See their:
  - Profile picture and bio
  - Post count
  - Followers/Following count
  - All their posts (grid view)
  - Video posts (reels view)

#### **Follow Users:**
- Tap the **"Follow"** button on their profile
- Their posts will appear in your feed
- They'll be notified (coming soon)

#### **Unfollow Users:**
- Tap the **"Following"** button to unfollow
- You'll stop seeing their posts in your feed

---

### 7. 💬 Direct Messages

Send private messages to other users (feature in development).

#### **Conversations:**
- See all your active conversations
- View message history
- See read/unread status
- Real-time message delivery

#### **Send Messages:**
1. Go to a user's profile
2. Tap **"Message"** button
3. Type your message
4. Tap send
5. Messages appear instantly

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### **Can't Upload Image?**
- Check camera/storage & formets permissions
- Ensure image isn't too large
- Try taking a new photo instead

### **Video Won't Upload?**
- Check video is under 10MB
- Ensure video is under 15 seconds (or use trimmer)
- Try MP4 format
- Check internet connection

### **App Crashes?**
- Close and reopen the app
- Check for app updates
- Clear app cache
- Reinstall if needed

### **Profile Picture Not Updating?**
- Wait a few seconds for upload
- Check internet connection
- Try a different image
- Ensure image is clear and not corrupted
